Whisper's architecture in 3 parts

Your browser holds the keys, QoreX signs, and the WhisperMailbox contract on QoreChain stores every envelope with an index per address. Signatures are checked against QoreChain's PQC key registry.

Questions

What people ask before their first message.

Who can read my Whisper messages?

Only the holder of the recipient's inbox key, and you through your own sealed copy. The chain shows who wrote to whom, when, and the padded size.

What does a Whisper message cost?

A 0.001 QOR fee plus gas. With QoreX's window transaction a send costs about 0.06 test QOR today.

What do I need to use Whisper?

QoreX on testnet with an Ethereum account (coin 60). Checking a signed message on the proof page needs no wallet.

Can strangers flood my Whisper inbox?

Turn on request mode: unknown addresses can send one contact request until you accept them, and the contract refuses blocked senders.

Is Whisper on mainnet?

No. It runs on QoreChain testnet until the contract has an outside review.

Whisper SDK and message button

Add one element and one script tag, and your page gets a button that opens Whisper addressed to the creator, with a draft.

The same SDK sends, reads and verifies messages in your own code, in TypeScript, with 29 tests.
